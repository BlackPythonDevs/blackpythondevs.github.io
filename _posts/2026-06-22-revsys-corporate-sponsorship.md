We're happy to announce that REVSYS (Revolution Systems LLC) has agreed to become a corporate sponsor of Black Python Devs.

This sponsorship will help secure infrastructure funding for Black Python Devs to operate in 2026 and beyond.

We've had the pleasure of working with REVSYS in the past, and we're particularly interested in learning how they've found success working with individual developers in LATAM and Africa, where members in those regions are often hoping to find work and can supplement income time-to-time with partnerships like these.

Our corporate sponsorship program allows REVSYS to promote job opportunities directly in our Discord, promotion of REVSYS campaigns, events, and initiatives to our community, the direct communication access to our BPD Council, as well as the ability to host dedicated events for BPD leaders and members.

This infrastructure support is the resource that funds subscription, BPD event, and event support costs. It is not intended to replace those orgs sponsoring events as we hope our partnerships with our sponsors will help them to establish their own relationships with the communities and leaders that we support.

Thank you again to REVSYS for their decision to sponsor the Python community and Black Python Devs.
