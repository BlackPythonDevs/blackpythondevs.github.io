We're excited to announce that Black Python Devs has partnered with [Reuven Lerner](https://lernerpython.com/) and [LernerPython](https://lernerpython.com/).

LernerPython offers Python training and courses that have helped developers around the world level up their skills.

What makes LernerPython special is their commitment to accessibility. LernerPython practices amazing region-based pricing for EVERYONE, meaning that the cost of training is linked to regional income levels. This ensures that high-quality Python education is affordable no matter where you live.

As part of this partnership, LernerPython has agreed to offer discounts for those connected in the Black Python Devs community Discord.

If you're a member of our community, keep an eye on our Discord for details on how to access these discounts.

You can view this and other partnerships with course content creators on our [partnerships page](/support#partnerships).

Thank you to Reuven Lerner and LernerPython for partnering with Black Python Devs and for their commitment to making Python education accessible to everyone.
