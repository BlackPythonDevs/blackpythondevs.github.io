We're excited to announce the results of our regional executorship election!

**Congratulations to our newly elected regional executives:**

- **US/Canada** — Nick Mouh
- **LATAM** — Denny Perez
- **Africa** — Emmanuel "Nuel" Ugwu
- **Western** — Damilola Oladele
- **Eastern** — Brayan Kai
- **Southern** — Kudzayi Bamhare

These exceptional leaders were selected from BPD Council members who self-nominated and were chosen by our existing executive team based on their vision, commitment, and dedication to our community.

We want to extend our heartfelt thanks to everyone who nominated themselves for this opportunity. Your willingness to step up and lead inspires us all. Whether you're elected this cycle or not, we encourage you to continue contributing to Black Python Devs—there are many ways to make an impact and be a leader in our community beyond the executive team.

As we welcome this new leadership, we're grateful to our outgoing executive team for their invaluable contributions and stewardship. We're thrilled to move forward with this accomplished team and can't wait to see what we'll accomplish together!
