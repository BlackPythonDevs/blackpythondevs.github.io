Congratulations to
[Verónica Rodríguez Viveros](https://www.linkedin.com/in/veronica-rodriguez-viveros/)
for recognition and consideration for the BPD Council!

Verónica Rodríguez Viveros is an Afro-Colombian technology leader, community
builder, and product strategist with more than 10 years of experience leading
digital products across fintech, banking, logistics, cybersecurity, and identity
verification. Currently a Senior Product Manager at Twilio, she has built
and scaled products serving global markets while driving innovation, growth, and
operational excellence. Originally trained as an economist, Verónica combines
strong business acumen with technical expertise to translate complex challenges
into impactful digital solutions. Her work spans Colombia, the United States,
Brazil, Portugal, and Mexico, and she collaborates with cross-functional teams
to launch products that deliver measurable business and customer value.

Beyond her corporate career, Verónica is deeply committed to expanding
opportunities for underrepresented communities in technology. She is the Co-
founder of Digital Diaspora an initiative dedicated to increasing
Afro-descendant representation in the tech industry through mentorship,
education, and professional development. She is also
[Women Techmakers Ambassador](https://www.technovation.org/women-techmakers/), a
leader within [Pioneras](https://www.pionerasdev.org/), a YLAI Fellow of the
U.S. Department of State, and an active advocate for diversity, equity, and
inclusion in the technology ecosystem. Through speaking engagements, community
programs, and international collaborations, she works to create pathways for
economic mobility and leadership for women, Afro descendants, and emerging
talent across Latin America.

The BPD council is the sounding board for decisions that need to be made in
Black Python Devs. They are the advisory group that shares how our actions can
impact their communities. The council is made up of organizers, BPD leaders, and
those in leadership positions around the Python world.

Congratulations again to Veronica!
