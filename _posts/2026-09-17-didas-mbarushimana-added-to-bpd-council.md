Congratulations to
[Didas Mbarushimana](https://www.linkedin.com/in/didasmbarushimana/) for
recognition and consideration for the BPD Council!

Didas Mbarushimana is a Software Engineer, open-source community leader, and
developer advocate based in Kigali, Rwanda. He is an active leader in the
African open source ecosystem, working to build inclusive developer communities,
support open source contributors, and create opportunities for developers to
connect, learn, and collaborate.

Didas leads Open Source Kigali and Django Rwanda, helping create spaces where
developers can build skills, contribute to open source projects, and connect
more with the global technology ecosystem. He is passionate about open source,
artificial intelligence, and developer communities, with a particular focus on
empowering African developers to build, contribute, and collaborate globally.

The BPD council is the sounding board for decisions that need to be made in
Black Python Devs. They advise on how our actions can impact their communities.
The council is made up of organizers, BPD leaders, and those in leadership
positions around the Python world.

Congratulations again to Didas!
