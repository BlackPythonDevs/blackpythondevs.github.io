We're happy to announce that Anaconda has agreed to become a corporate sponsor of Black Python Devs.

Our corporate sponsorship program allows Anaconda to promote job opportunities directly in our Discord, promotion of Anaconda campaigns, events, and initiatives to our community, as well as the ability to host dedicated events for BPD leaders and members.

This sponsorship will help secure infrastructure funding for Black Python Devs to operate in 2026 and beyond. Our infrastructure budget is the resource that supports subscription and event costs. It is not intended (but potentially can be used) to sponsor events as we hope our partnerships with our sponsors will help them to establish relationships with the communities and leaders that we support.

Thank you to Anaconda for their decision to sponsor the Python community and Black Python Devs.
