## Overview

On July 24th, 2026, Black Python Devs hosted their 2nd Leadership Summit, this time extending beyond the BPD community and serving as a resource for the Python community leaders.

Approximately 30 of Python's conference organizers, meetup leaders, authors, business owners, and those inspiring to fill their shoes someday gathered at Cleveland State University on the eve of PyOhio. There we shared, our burning questions, experiences, successes and failures hoping to learn from one another.

## Keynotes

International speaker, author, and data-monger Bonny Mcclain gave a wonderful lesson on how to tell unique stories around open search data. We explored the movement of bears, the sounds of rice whales, and the biology of islands threatened by millionaire investments, and how humanization and polarization in the AI conversation can skew our opinion of information that may be flawed from the original prompt.

In the afternoon, we heard from Joy Hopkins who shared with us how we can lead from the front, or the middle. And how we can prevent the overload with quantification. She reminded us that humans are _pack animals_ and how we could lead, even when no one is following.

Both talks emphasized the importance of trying to form a complete picture using data and presenting in a relatable, and digestible way.

## Community Talks

This year, we opened our community talks to a CFP. We had amazing community presenters Jon Banafato, Lazouich Ford (BPD's North American Executor, 2026), and PyOhio organizer Kattni.

Jon kicked off the talks with _a metaphor about chairs_, where we learned how the jobs that focus around stacking, unstacking, and setting out chairs, and other volunteer services become a way for people to get involved in your event. He reminded us the power of "Wanna help?".

Lazouich shared with us how the wisdom of his parents helped with him taking the reigns as the North American Executor for Black Python Devs. He reminded us that "It's who you know", and when being a leader has us going to "Crazy," we should "Just Keep Living".

![Recap Slide from Lazouich's Community Talk](/assets/images/bdls-pyohio-laz-community-preso.webp)

Kattni gave a powerful talk about how to let your community be self-sufficient on things. She also covered how to help those who are the "face" of a project, be able to get out of that celebrity space.
