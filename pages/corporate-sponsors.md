# Become a Corporate Sponsor

Black Python Devs Supports Events and Communities Across the North and South America as well as Europe and Africa that help with providing access affordable learning, community and networking opportunities for Black, African, Coloured and Afro-Latino communities around the world. 

Your corporate support gives us the ability to send local leaders to events to represent Black Python Devs, raise awareness, fund-raise, and more. It also covers BPD infrastructure, marketing materials and more. 

Providing corporate support starts at $5,000. This sponsorship would include the following:

- auto-posting of job opportunities to the BPD discord and newsletter (Limited to Ripling and Greenhouse job boards). 
- inclusion of your compoany Logo on blackpythondevs.com on our homepage and our supporters section. 
- presentation of your company on printer promotional materials at official BPD events
- invites and opportunities to connect at official BPD events
- the hosting of a sponsored BPD virtual meetup to present the topic of your choice.

![Jay Miller with Corporate Sponsor JetBrains](/assets/images/jetbrains-party.jpg)

## What this doesn't do

It is intentional that funds from corporate sponsors are not primarily used to sponsor events. We believe that it's important that our corporate sponsors establish direct relationships with the communities that we support. We're happy to help with that but cannot make up for the immense support that you can provide to them.
